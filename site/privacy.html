Privacy Policy

Google Play Certified Compliant
Application: ArrowMint - The arrow game  •  Package ID: com.arrowmint.thearrowgame  •  Effective Date: August 17, 2026

Welcome to ArrowMint - The arrow game ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, enjoyable gaming experience. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play ArrowMint.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

ArrowMint is designed with privacy-first principles. We do not collect or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), such as:

  • Your real name, address, or phone number
  • Your email address or contact list
  • Your precise GPS physical location
  • Microphone, camera, or biometric data
  • Payment card or banking details

2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

To support free gameplay and deliver optional rewarded video ads (such as +1 Life revives or booster unlocks), our app integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) and Google Play Services.

These third-party service providers may automatically collect non-personal technical device data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): Used by AdMob to deliver relevant or frequency-capped advertisements.
  • Device & OS Specifications: Device model, Android OS version, language, screen resolution, and mobile carrier.
  • Performance & Diagnostic Data: Crash reports and technical diagnostic logs to optimize performance and prevent crashes.

For further details regarding how Google processes information, please review the Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Policies.

3. Local Device Data & Storage

All game progression, unlocked levels, high scores, collected Sparks, equipped themes, and booster counts are stored locally on your device using secure on-device storage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to uninstall the app or clear its application cache.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

ArrowMint is a family-friendly casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. All advertising units are configured in compliance with Google Play Families and Child-Directed Policy requirements.

5. Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising

You can easily opt out of personalized advertisements or reset your Advertising ID directly on your Android device:

  • Open your device Settings
  • Navigate to Google → Ads
  • Tap Delete Advertising ID or toggle Opt out of Ads Personalization

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security safeguards. All communications between third-party SDKs and Google servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated Effective Date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or your data rights, please contact our team at:
